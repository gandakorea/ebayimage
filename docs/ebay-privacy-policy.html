Privacy Policy

Sihooshop Australia Listing Manager · Effective September 8, 2026

This private seller tool connects to eBay to prepare and manage Sihooshop listings.

Data used

The tool processes eBay account authorization credentials and listing information needed to create and manage the account owner's listings. It does not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

Storage and sharing

Credentials and working listing data are kept in systems controlled by the account owner. Data is shared with eBay only when needed to perform an action requested by the account owner.

Account deletion notices

The notification endpoint acknowledges eBay marketplace account deletion notices without storing their payloads. If related personal data is held elsewhere, it is deleted as required by eBay policy and applicable law.

Retention and security

Credentials are retained only while the integration is in use and are protected as secrets. Access can be revoked through eBay at any time.

Contact

Privacy questions can be directed to the owner of the Sihooshop eBay seller account.
